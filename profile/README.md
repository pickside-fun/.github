# PICKSIDE

**Pick a side. Follow the action.**

PICKSIDE turns competing narratives into team matches. World news and crypto markets become the arena: choose a team, follow live data, and see which side comes out ahead.

**[Play PICKSIDE](https://pickside.fun)** · **[Follow @picksidefun on X](https://x.com/picksidefun)**

## Two ways to play

- **News** — politics, technology, and world events face off as teams, with scores driven by prediction-market data.
- **Degens** — fast-paced memecoin team battles, where live price changes drive the action.

PICKSIDE is an evolving MVP. The current **SIDE balance is an internal game currency**, not an issued on-chain token or a promise of cash value.

## Build with us

We welcome players, builders, and communities with ideas for new narratives, agent experiences, and collaborations. Try the game and [reach out on X](https://x.com/picksidefun) with feedback or a proposal.

The application source is currently private. This page is our public front door — follow along as PICKSIDE grows.
